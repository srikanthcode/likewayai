# How to Fix Vercel Deployment

## Step 1: Go to Vercel Dashboard
Open: https://vercel.com/srikanthcodes-projects/likewayai1/deployments

## Step 2: Find the Latest Deployment
Look for the most recent deployment at the top

## Step 3: Click "..." (3 dots) on the right
Click the three dots menu

## Step 4: Click "Redeploy"
Click "Redeploy" from the menu

## Step 5: Confirm Redeployment
Click "Redeploy" again to confirm

---

## Wait 1-2 Minutes for Deployment

Then refresh your page: https://likewayai1.vercel.app

You should see the NEW pricing page with:
- Core (Free)
- Go (₹299)
- Plus (₹799)
- Pro (₹1,999)

---

## If Still Not Working

Try these steps:

1. Clear browser cache:
   - Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
   
2. Or open in incognito/private window:
   - Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+N
   - Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+P

3. Check Vercel deployment logs for errors
